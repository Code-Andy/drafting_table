# 2026-09-03 — v0.9.7 Release: Gap-Closing Bucket Fill, Paper Space Maximization & Pencil Pro Radial Wheel

## Overview

Version **0.9.7** (build **16**) delivers:
1. **Intelligent Gap-Closing Bucket Fill**: Fast morphological raster flood fill bridging line gaps with configurable error margins and under-stroke bleed.
2. **Apple Pencil Pro Squeeze Radial Wheel**: Circular contextual tool menu popping up centered around the pencil point on the glass, replacing tabbed action sheet popups.
3. **Paper Space Maximization**: Complete removal of the top navigation bar and title ribbon, allowing paper canvas to occupy 100% of the screen.
4. **Tool Rail & Quick Color Palette**: Top-left hamburger menu (`≡`), active swatch button, and 8 quick-palette pen colors for instant drafting.
5. **Parity with Original Drafting Table**: Floating Undo/Redo pill chip over top-left paper, `DOCS ∨` button at top of Pages rail, and aligned tool groupings.

---

## 1. Gap-Closing Bucket Fill

- **Morphological Gap Closing**: Offscreen rasterization with 2-pass separable obstacle dilation (`gapRadius = (gapSize + 1) / 2`) prevents flood fills from leaking through line gaps.
- **Under-Stroke Bleed**: Filled regions expand by `gapRadius + bleed` to eliminate unpainted white halos and cleanly seat color underneath outline strokes.
- **Ear-Clipping Triangulation**: General ear-clipping algorithm in `DTMetalRenderer.mm` renders concave and convex bucket fill polygons without self-intersection artifacts.
- **Sub-Tool Settings**: Floating `bucketSubToolBar` displays when the Bucket tool is active:
  - **Gap Error Margin**: `0px`, `2px`, `4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`.
  - **Bleed / Expansion**: `0px`, `1px`, `2px`, `4px`.
- **First-Class Stroke Model**: Bucket fills are stored as native strokes (`DTTool::Bucket = 7`), supporting undo/redo, move/scale/rotate transforms, serialization, and PNG/PDF export.

---

## 2. Apple Pencil Pro Squeeze: Circular Radial Wheel (`CircularRadialMenuView.swift`)

- **Fluid Radial Menu**: Spawns centered at the Apple Pencil Pro hover point or touch point on the glass with screen-edge clamping and spring animation.
- **Radial Tools**: 12 tools arranged in a circular dial: Brush, Eraser, Bucket, Shade, Line, Rectangle, Circle, Ellipse, Select, Lasso, Color, and Undo.
- **Central Preview Disc & Haptics**: Shows active tool preview and fires tactile feedback via `HapticFeedbackService` on squeeze and selection.

---

## 3. UI Parity & Paper Space Maximization

- **Zero Top Chrome**: Navigation bar hidden (`setNavigationBarHidden(true)`); 40pt top ribbon eliminated. Canvas extends to `root.topAnchor`.
- **Main Menu (`≡`)**: Integrated into the top of the left tool rail for Gallery, Rename, Export, Import, Settings, and Clear.
- **Quick Pen Colors**: 8 primitive palette colors (Black, Ink, Red, Blue, Green, Amber, Gray, White) positioned right on the tool rail below the active swatch button.
- **Floating Undo/Redo Chip**: Floating pill over the top-left canvas matching the upstream layout.
- **`DOCS ∨` Header**: Added to top of Pages rail to open the Notebook Gallery.
