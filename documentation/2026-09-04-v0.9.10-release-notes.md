# 2026-09-04 — v0.9.10 Release: Paper Sheet Bounds & Shadow, Post-Stroke Grid, Top Bar Brush/Palette, Circle/Ellipse Grid Snap & Unbounded Stroke Length

## Overview

Version **0.9.10** (build **19**) delivers:
1. **Concrete Paper Sheet with Drop Shadow & Infinite Desk Canvas**: Replaced the infinite void grid with a concrete paper document quad positioned on a warm dark drafting desk surface (`#1A1714`). Configurable paper sizes (iPad Native 1536×2048, US Letter 1700×2200, A4 Drafting 1754×2480, Square 2048×2048, Wide 16:9 2560×1440) with drop shadow and outline, supporting infinite pan and zoom across the desk surface with instant "Reset View" centering.
2. **Post-Stroke Grid Rendering (Eraser Never Erases Grid)**: Fixed the grid cut-through issue where eraser strokes would erase or punch holes through grid lines. The document grid and pixel grid are now rendered **after** all drawing and eraser strokes, strictly clipped within the paper sheet boundaries (`[0, paperWidth] × [0, paperHeight]`).
3. **Dedicated Top Tool Bar for Instant 1-Tap Colors & Brush Controls**:
   - Undo and Redo buttons.
   - Active color swatch button (tap opens full HSV color picker).
   - 10 Quick 1-tap color swatches (Black, Ink, Dark Brown, Charcoal, Warm Gray, White, Drafting Red, Drafting Blue, Drafting Green, Drafting Amber) with live active-state highlighting.
   - 4 My Slots (tap to use, long-press to save).
   - Full Color Picker shortcut button.
   - Quick Brush Size slider with live pt readout.
   - Quick Brush Opacity slider with live percentage readout.
   - Paper Size menu button.
   - Reset View & center paper button.
4. **Clean & Compact Vertical Tool Rail**: Removed crowded color palettes and slot grids from the left tool rail, leaving a focused, compact tool panel dedicated to Draw, Vector, Select, and Panel toggles.
5. **Circle & Ellipse Center-Mode Grid Snap Fix**: Fixed distortion, jumping, and stroke doubling when drawing circles and ellipses in center-mode with grid snap. Grid snapping is now applied to center anchor and radius/axes, and coordinate inversion in Metal is strictly scoped to live in-progress strokes.
6. **Complete Removal of Stroke & Erase Length Maximums**: Removed all hardcoded stroke/erase point limits (`kMaximumInputPoints = 512`, `kMaximumSmoothedPoints = 1024` in `DTMetalRenderer.mm`, and `4096` in `DTEngineBridge.mm`), allowing continuous, unbounded drawing and erasing without truncation.

## 1. Paper Sheet Bounds & Grid Layering

- **Warm Desk Surface**: Canvas MTKView clear color set to `#1A1714` (`MTLClearColorMake(0.102, 0.090, 0.078, 1.0)`).
- **Paper Geometry**: Document space quad `[0, paperWidth] × [0, paperHeight]` rendered with a soft 12pt drop shadow, warm paper tone, and crisp hairline border.
- **Post-Stroke Grid**: Grid lines and pixel grid are rendered in document space after all stroke rasterization. When the eraser paints the paper surface tone over artwork, grid lines remain pristine and unbroken across the sheet.

---

## 2. Top Bar & 1-Tap Color Switching

- **Zero-Friction Palette**: 10 essential drafting swatches sit prominently on the horizontal top bar, allowing single-tap color changes while drawing.
- **Visual Feedback**: Selected swatches dynamically scale with a bold ink border.
- **Direct Sliders**: Brush size (1.0–40.0 pt) and opacity (5%–100%) can be adjusted immediately from the top bar without opening submenus.

---

## 3. Circle/Ellipse Center-Mode & Grid Snap

- **Snapping**: `touchesBegan` and `touchesEnded` snap center point, radius, and axes to grid intervals when snap-to-grid is enabled.
- **Canonical Rendering**: Active strokes render interactively from center-to-perimeter in Metal during drag; committed strokes render from bounding box geometry without doubling.
